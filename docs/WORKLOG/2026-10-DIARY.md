### Fix mod archive precedence over stock loose files (#335)
- Diagnosed issue #335 where Shockwave mod Skirmish AI was completely inactive when assigned new generals (Armor, Salvage, Special Weapons).
- Discovered that retail Zero Hour installs ship stock loose files in `Data/Scripts/` (e.g. `SkirmishScripts.scb`) that shadowed custom scripts packaged inside mod archives (`!Shw_scripts.big`), because `TheLocalFileSystem` always prioritized loose files over archives.
